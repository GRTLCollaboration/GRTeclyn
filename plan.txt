That gives you three regimes:
0 to -1: control / weak perturbation / likely no collapse
-2 to -3: threshold region
-3.5 to -4: strong collapse, larger junk and earlier burst
If you want a cleaner threshold study, start with:
0.0, -1.5, -2.0, -2.5, -3.0
Then refine between the two neighboring values where behavior changes.
