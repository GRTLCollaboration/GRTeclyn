# Trajectory search campaigns

Every automated search behind the article, oldest first. Each folder is a
light, git-friendly extract of one campaign directory from the gitignored
`/runs` tree: genomes, scoreboards, per-eval diagnostics and solver residuals,
but none of the heavy binaries (no `initial_data.gridinit` at ~530 MB per eval,
no `small_data/metric_stack`, no plotfiles or frames).

Regenerate or refresh every pack here with

```bash
bash research/neuralspacetime/pack_search_campaigns.sh
```

It is safe to re-run at any time, including mid-campaign. Per-eval directories
are additive: the live tree keeps only the top few evals and is trimmed by hand
to reclaim disk, so for a pruned eval **this pack is the last surviving copy**
and a re-pack will never delete it.

## The lineage

The five campaigns form one chain — each is warm-started from the one above it,
so a genome can be followed from the first pump-era elite to the current
champion.

| # | Pack | Search | Objective | Evals | Best path saving | Champion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`qball-trajectory-pump-seed-search/`](qball-trajectory-pump-seed-search/) | MAP-Elites | pump-era, `ftl_lifetime` descriptor | 200 | 22.15 % | eval 13 |
| 2 | [`qball-trajectory-evolving-geodesic-shortcut-search/`](qball-trajectory-evolving-geodesic-shortcut-search/) | MAP-Elites | `f_geo_max` (persistence-gated) | 400 | 25.02 % | eval 322 |
| 3 | [`qball-trajectory-geodesic-depth-search/`](qball-trajectory-geodesic-depth-search/) | MAP-Elites | `f_geo_depth` (raw depth) | 200 | 45.89 % | eval 7 |
| 4 | [`qball-trajectory-cmaes-refinement/`](qball-trajectory-cmaes-refinement/) | CMA-ES | `f_geo_depth` | 200 | **48.38 %** (47.97 % clean-branch) | eval 195 |
| 5 | [`qball-trajectory-fgeo-max-refinement/`](qball-trajectory-fgeo-max-refinement/) | CMA-ES | `f_geo_max` | 200 | **35.94 %** | eval 193 |

**Path saving is comparable across every row; scores are not.** The score is
objective-specific — a `f_geo_depth` score of 4862 and a `f_geo_max` score of
3666 measure different things and must never be compared. Read the path-saving
column instead.

### Why two lineages, and which one is the headline

Rows 3–4 optimise **raw depth**: how far a light ray gets ahead of its flat
twin, with no requirement that the effect last. They reach the deepest numbers
in the study (48.38 %), and they are the Pareto/ceiling exhibit.

Rows 2 and 5 optimise **gated depth**: the same shortcut, but multiplied by a
persistence requirement, so a one-frame spike scores nothing. This is the
article's agenda — a shortcut that exists only instantaneously is not a
transport claim — and row 5 is the **headline campaign**. Its champion is the
genome promoted to paper resolution.

Row 1 is the ancestor. It predates the evolving-geodesic measurement entirely
and optimised the pump; its retained elites are what
`scripts/campaigns/qball_trajectory/run_fgeo.sh` warm-starts from by default,
which is why it is kept rather than deleted.

## What is in each pack

Layout is the same everywhere; items marked ᴳ are generated by the pack script
and rebuilt on every run, the rest are hand-written or copied verbatim.

- `README.md`, `CAMPAIGN_RESULTS.md` — what the campaign did and what it found.
  Never touched by the pack script.
- `analysis/` ᴳ
  - `per_eval.csv` — one row per evaluation: generation, status, score, path
    saving, emission and arrival times, constraint residuals, exotic-matter
    integral, energy-condition minima. A resumed campaign re-scores the evals
    that were in flight when it stopped; those rows collapse to the
    best-scoring one, which is what the optimiser itself kept.
  - `per_generation.csv` — generational campaigns only (CMA-ES): best and
    median per generation, and how many evals the constraint gate rejected.
    MAP-Elites is steady-state and has no generations, so it has no such file.
- `genomes/` — the genomes that matter, each with its measurements. Present
  only where a campaign has a genome worth citing on its own.
- `logs/` ᴳ — progress lines lifted out of the multi-hundred-MB launch log.
- `run/` — the campaign directory itself, minus heavy binaries:
  - `trajectory.jsonl` — every evaluation with its full genome. This is the
    complete record; an eval whose directory was pruned still has its genome
    and all its scored components here.
  - `metadata.json` — configuration, search-space bounds, gates, seed.
  - `ftl_champions.json`, `ftl_retention.jsonl` — the per-metric record
    holders and the moment each record changed hands.
  - `archive.json` — MAP-Elites only: the descriptor grid and its occupants.
  - `eval_NNNNNN/` — the retained evals: `params.txt`, `score.json`,
    `metadata.json` (the genome as run), `small_data/` (emission sweep in
    `evolving_geodesic.json`, plus the FTL and confinement time series),
    `data/` (constraint norms, energy conditions, curvature invariants,
    collapse diagnostics), `grtresna/Ham_and_Mom_errors.txt` (solver residual
    history) and `postload_gate/` (the evidence the eval was admitted
    honestly).

## Which evals are kept, and why so few

A campaign runs 200–400 evaluations but keeps only a handful of directories.
Three rules decide, and they overlap:

1. **Top-N by score** — `KEEP_TOP_EVAL_DIRS`, 10 for the CMA-ES runs and 3 for
   the MAP-Elites runs.
2. **Record holders** — the retention feature additionally keeps whichever eval
   currently holds the record for *each separate metric* (path saving,
   persistence, peak operational speed, …). Different evals own different
   records, so this adds several directories that the top-N rule would not.
3. **The newest generation**, until it has been ranked.

Everything else is deleted as the campaign runs, because each directory carries
a ~530 MB constraint-solved initial-data grid. The live trees were additionally
trimmed to their top 5 by hand on 2026-08-19 to reclaim ~14 GB; the packs here
still hold every eval that was ever packed, including several the live tree no
longer has.

Nothing is lost by that trimming that the article depends on: `trajectory.jsonl`
carries the full genome and every scored component for all 200–400 evaluations,
so any eval can be re-run from it exactly.

## Reproducing a run

Launcher, environment, monitoring, resume and stop commands are in each pack's
`CAMPAIGN_RESULTS.md`. To replay a packed eval at any resolution:

```bash
cd grteclyn-wrapper
.venv/bin/python scripts/campaigns/hq/replay_eval.py \
  ../results/matter-first-automated-discovery-of-transient-spacetime-shortcuts/search/<pack>/run/eval_NNNNNN \
  --name <label> --runs-dir ../runs/neuralspacetime/hq \
  --gpu 0 --n-full 256 --l-full 128 --stop-time 64 \
  --objective-mode <f_geo_max|f_geo_depth> --evolving-geodesic --grtresna-ranks 1
```

The pump runs for the entire simulation in every campaign here. That is the
convention, it is enforced at launch, and a replay that stops the pump must
declare itself a deliberate control — see the wrapper README, "Pump convention
(enforced at launch)".
